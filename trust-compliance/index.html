Accepting a payment is only the start

Screen. Assess. Route. Review

Clean Settlement is Cryptix's screening layer for incoming crypto transactions

Its job is to assess transaction risk before funds continue through the payment flow

Screen

Cryptix analyzes available blockchain data related to the transaction and the wallets involved.

Assess

Risk signals may include:

  • Links to sanctions lists and blocklists
  • Interaction with mixers and similar fund-obfuscation services
  • Сonnections to restricted or high-risk services
  • Jurisdictional risks
  • Wallet and transaction risk indicators
  • Applicable internal rules

Routing

More than 99% of transactions are screened automatically. Transactions with elevated risk signals are routed for additional review.

Additional review

The resulting risk profile determines whether a transaction can be processed automatically or requires further action under the applicable compliance process.

Cryptix uses blockchain risk-scoring systems and works with Crystal Blockchain on AML compliance and crypto transaction risk management.

What Clean Settlement does not certify

Clean Settlement does not certify that funds are “clean” under all circumstances.

It does not rule out additional review by a bank, regulator, payment partner, or counterparty. It also does not replace the merchant’s own obligations related to AML, sanctions screening, customer due diligence, or regulatory reporting.

Share sender and recipient data where required

Travel Rule requirements can require regulated virtual asset service providers (VASPs) to exchange sender and recipient information for a transfer.

Cryptix supports Travel Rule workflows, including interactions with VASPs. Applicability depends on the type of transfer, counterparties, and jurisdiction.

The process may include

  • Identifying the counterparty VASP
  • Сollecting the required sender and recipient data
  • Exchanging information through the available Travel Rule workflow
  • Linking the information to the corresponding transfer

FAQ

Does Clean Settlement guarantee that funds are clean?

No. Clean Settlement screens transactions and applies risk controls within the Cryptix payment flow. It does not provide a universal legal determination of the source of funds or guarantee that another institution will not request additional information.

What happens when a transaction is flagged as higher risk?

Transactions with elevated risk signals are routed for additional review. The next steps depend on the identified risk factors, applicable policies, and legal requirements.

Does Cryptix replace the merchant's compliance system?

No. Cryptix's controls are part of the payment infrastructure. Merchants remain responsible for its customers, its activity, and its regulatory obligations.

Is Cryptix available in every country?

No. Company onboarding and product availability depend on jurisdictional restrictions and Cryptix's current restricted-countries policy.

Who provides regulated virtual currency services?

IZOLDA PAY INC. is the primary and exclusive provider of regulated virtual currency exchange and transfer services for newly onboarded clients. RSG UNIVERSAL PAYMENTS LTD. acts solely as the technical platform operator.

Can platform and API access be restricted?

Yes. Available controls include two-factor authentication, API method permissions, IP restrictions, and withdrawal address allowlists.

Does Cryptix support the Travel Rule?

Yes. The Cryptix API includes Travel Rule functionality. Whether it applies to a specific transaction depends on the transfer type, counterparties, and jurisdiction
