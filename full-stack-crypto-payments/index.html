Convert

Convert incoming funds into the currency your business needs.

With Cryptix, you can:

  • Keep funds in the original cryptocurrency
  • Convert incoming payments automatically
  • Convert funds in the Dashboard
  • Run crypto-to-crypto conversions
  • Convert crypto to fiat where available
  • Execute large-volume OTC trades

Automatic conversion simplifies balance management and reduces exposure to crypto price volatility. For large volumes, negotiated terms and dedicated OTC trade support are available.

Crypto in. Crypto out

Accept crypto payments, screen incoming transactions, convert funds when needed, and pay out to crypto wallets.

Best for:

  • User deposits and withdrawals
  • Partner payouts
  • Affiliate programs
  • Mass payouts
  • Crypto-native business models

Crypto in. Fiat to your bank account

Accept crypto, convert it automatically or on demand, and withdraw fiat to a company bank account.

Best for:

  • Businesses that want to accept crypto while keeping revenue and reporting in fiat

Fund in crypto. Pay out in fiat

Fund your balance in crypto, convert the required amount, and send fiat payouts through available cards and alternative payment methods.

Best for:

  • User withdrawals
  • Partner payouts
  • Affiliate programs
  • Contractor payouts
  • Mass payouts

Large volume. OTC trades

Execute a negotiated OTC trade, then use the funds for payouts or withdrawals.

Best for:

  • Best for transactions where the volume, currency pair, or execution terms require a negotiated trade.

Fiat in. Crypto to a wallet

Let users buy crypto through available partner on-ramp infrastructure.

  • Users pay in fiat and receive crypto in the wallet they provide.
  • Availability depends on partner coverage, jurisdiction, and merchant configuration.

Risk management and security are built into the full payment flow — from merchant onboarding to transaction screening and payouts.

Company verification

As part of onboarding, every merchant completes KYB. Available products, limits, and fiat capabilities depend on the verification level.

Transaction screening

Incoming crypto transactions undergo AML and KYT screening before funds move on.

Payout controls

Withdrawal address allowlists and two-factor authentication help protect withdrawals, refunds, and payouts.

API controls
