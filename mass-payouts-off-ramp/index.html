Top up. Convert. Pay out. Track.

  1. 1

    Top up

    Use your available Cryptix balance for outgoing transactions. Depending on the merchant configuration, funds can be held in crypto or fiat.

  2. 2

    Convert

    If a payout requires a different currency, convert funds using the available Cryptix conversion options.

    The platform supports crypto-to-crypto and crypto-to-fiat conversions

  3. 3

    Pay out

    Create a one-off or mass payout and choose an available route for the recipient.

  4. 4

    Track

    Track every payout through statuses in the Dashboard or callbacks via the API.

For mass payouts, you can use external IDs and callback URLs. Each payout within a batch keeps its own status, while the batch shows the total amount and the number of successful and failed payouts.

Most launches take around three weeks. Timing depends on the selected products, integration method, and payout routes.
