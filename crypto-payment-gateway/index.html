Choose how you integrate

API and H2H

Integrate crypto payments directly into your product.

Create payments, generate customer addresses, link transactions to internal orders with external IDs, and receive status updates through callbacks. Best for businesses that need full control over the customer journey and payment logic.

iFrame

Embed the Cryptix checkout directly into your website and keep customers within your interface. Best for teams that want a faster launch without building the full checkout experience from scratch.

Hosted checkout

Send customers to a Cryptix-hosted checkout page, then return them to your product after payment. Best for teams that need a complete payment flow with minimal frontend development.

Payment links

Create a payment link and share it by email, messenger, or any other channel. No website or custom checkout is required. Best for direct sales, sales teams, invoicing, and one-off payments.

Dedicated deposit addresses

Assign each customer a dedicated crypto address and track top-ups without creating a new payment page for every transfer. Best for wallets, trading platforms, gaming balances, and other account-based products.
